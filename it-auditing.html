IT Auditing

Protect your enterprise with in-depth cybersecurity checks, compliance reviews, network vulnerability scans, and security reports.

Robust Cybersecurity, Compliance, and Risk Management

A single security vulnerability can cost millions and damage your brand's reputation for years. At Skybertech, our IT Auditing team conducts exhaustive, independent checks on your hardware, software, networks, and internal policies to identify compliance gaps, detect vulnerabilities, and formulate remediation blueprints.

We believe in a proactive audit methodology. Instead of simple checklist audits, we conduct simulated intrusion attempts (penetration testing) and network stress tests to identify how your systems perform under load or attack. Our comprehensive reports offer both high-level executive summaries and itemized technical fixes for your engineering teams.

Core Offerings & Capabilities

Penetration Testing

Simulate realistic external and internal cyber threats to find weak entry points in web applications, cloud backends, and local firewalls.

Compliance Reviews

Align your security policies with international standards including ISO 27001, SOC2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, and industry-specific regulations.

Vulnerability Assessment

Establish automatic internal scans to detect outdated libraries, unpatched operating systems, and misconfigured ports before hackers exploit them.

Remediation Blueprints

Receive comprehensive, prioritize-by-risk reports showing exactly what systems to patch and how to update code to block vulnerabilities.
